# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 8 September 2026

## Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how **Nix Swahili** ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application, **Fabrizo** (the "Service"). Our app provides wallpaper showcases and media content for personal use.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information
We do **not** require users to create an account to use **Fabrizo**. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it to us (for example, by contacting our support team via email).

### 2. Automatically Collected Information
When you use the app, certain information may be collected automatically, including:
- Device type, model, hardware specs, and operating system version
- App usage data (such as wallpapers/videos viewed, downloads, and interaction metrics)
- Diagnostic details and crash logs
- IP address (used strictly for security, analytics, and service performance)

This data is collected in an aggregated or anonymized format and is used solely to optimize app performance and user experience.

### 3. Wallpapers and Media Content
**Fabrizo** allows you to browse, stream, and save wallpapers and videos to your device. Storage/media access is requested only when you choose to download or set a wallpaper/video directly through the app. We do not inspect, upload, or access your personal media or files.

## Permissions
To provide its core functionality, **Fabrizo** may request the following permissions:
- **Storage / Photos / Media access**: Required solely to save, apply, or download wallpapers and videos onto your device.
- **Internet access & Network status**: Required to fetch, stream, and load high-resolution wallpapers and video content.
- **Set Wallpaper**: Required to allow you to set wallpapers directly to your home screen or lock screen from within the app.

These permissions are used strictly for their stated functionality and never to harvest personal data.

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their respective policies for details on how they process data.

## How We Use Information
We use the collected information to:

- Maintain application security and guard against unauthorized or malicious activity

## Data Retention
We retain automatically collected diagnostic and usage information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law.
