# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Nix Swahili** ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the **Fabrizo** mobile application ("Fabrizo" or the "Service").

Fabrizo is a wallpaper and media application that allows users to browse, view, download, and set wallpapers and other media content on their devices.

By using Fabrizo, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the Service.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide

Fabrizo does not require you to create an account.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, postal address, phone number, or email address through normal use of the application.

If you voluntarily contact us, for example by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message. We use this information only to respond to your request, provide support, and address your inquiry.

### 2. Information Collected Automatically

When you use Fabrizo, certain technical and usage information may be collected automatically. This may include:

- Device model and manufacturer
- Android version and operating system information
- Application version
- IP address
- General application usage and interaction information
- Crash reports and diagnostic information
- Performance and technical information

This information may be used to:

- Operate and maintain the application
- Diagnose crashes and technical problems
- Improve application performance
- Monitor application stability
- Detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized activity
- Improve the user experience

We do not use automatically collected technical information to directly identify you.

## Wallpapers, Videos, and Device Files

Fabrizo may allow you to view, download, save, or set wallpapers and media content on your device.

When you choose to download or save content, the application may request the appropriate device permissions required to complete that action.

Fabrizo does **not** intentionally access, upload, inspect, or collect your personal photos, videos, documents, or other private files.

Any access to device storage or media is used only when necessary for a feature you explicitly request.

## Permissions

Depending on your device and the features you use, Fabrizo may request certain permissions.

### Internet Access

Internet access is required to:

- Load wallpapers and media content
- Stream or display online content
- Download content
- Communicate with our servers and third-party services

### Photos and Media / Storage

If required by your Android version, permission to access photos or media may be requested when you choose to save or download content to your device.

We do not use this permission to collect your personal media.

### Wallpaper

Fabrizo may use Android's wallpaper functionality to allow you to set content as your device wallpaper.

Permissions are used only for the functionality for which they are requested.
